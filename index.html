How to play

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD on a computer. You can also click and hold the mouse. On a phone, drag a finger on the screen like a joystick.
  • Sting: the bee automatically stings the nearest wasp.
  • Buzz wave: Space or the round button. It pushes back and damages every wasp around you.
  • Dash: Shift or the small button. A quick jump that dodges all damage.
  • Combo 🔥: defeat wasps in a row without getting hit and your score is multiplied (up to x3).
  • Power-ups: ⚡ double sting speed · 🛡️ shield · ❄️ freeze the wasps. They last a few seconds.
  • Honey 🍯: defeated wasps drop honey. Collect it to buy repairs between levels.
  • Upgrades: after each level you pick one upgrade.
  • Losing: when the hive or the bee runs out of health.
  • Bosses: big surprises are waiting on levels 5, 8 and 10…
  • Saving: the game saves at the start of every level. You can close it and come back with "Continue".
  • Endless mode ♾️: unlocks after you beat level 10.
  • Pause: P or Esc.

📖 The Fable of the Hive Keeper

Once, in a wide field of flowers, stood a small and quiet hive. All summer long the bees gathered nectar, drop by drop.

The wasps from the forest looked at the honey with envy and said: "Why work all summer, when we can simply take it?"

All the bees trembled, except one – the smallest of them. "I am small," she said, "but this home was built drop by drop, and I will guard it sting by sting."

And so she flew out alone to face the swarm…
