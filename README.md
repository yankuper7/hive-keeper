# Hive Keeper 🐝

A browser game: a bee defends her hive from wasps. 10 levels, upgrades, three bosses (levels 5, 8 and 10), three difficulty levels, combos, temporary power-ups, sounds and a high-score table.

## How to open
Double-click `index.html`. Nothing to install.

## Files
- `index.html` – structure and menus
- `style.css` – styling
- `game.js` – the whole game: logic, drawing, and sounds generated in code
- `hebrew-backup/` – a copy of the earlier Hebrew version

## Controls
- **Computer:** arrow keys or WASD (or hold the mouse), Space for the buzz wave, Shift to dash, P or Esc to pause, M to mute
- **Phone:** drag a finger like a joystick; the big button is the buzz wave and the small one is the dash

The game saves at the start of every level, and you can return with "Continue". Beating level 10 unlocks endless mode.

High scores and progress are stored in the browser (localStorage).
